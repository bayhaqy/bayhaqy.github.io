00 — About

From infrastructure engineering to enterprise AI leadership

I am an IT solutions leader working across enterprise infrastructure, data engineering, and applied AI. Today I head the IT, data, and AI function at PT MAP Active Adiperkasa Tbk, the public-listed sports retailer of the MAP Group, where I have grown from hands-on engineering into enterprise leadership over the past decade.

My day-to-day scope runs from medallion lakehouse architecture on Microsoft Fabric to multi-vendor Agentic AI (Microsoft Foundry, AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI), security operations, and enterprise-wide automation. The platform my team runs serves 15+ stakeholder groups — Operations, Merchandise, Supply Chain, Finance, HR, Audit, and Marketing — with governed data access for 500+ users and a 99.9%+ uptime commitment for mission-critical reporting.

Sentiment Analysis about Islamophobia during the Christchurch Attack on Social Media

TELKOMNIKA (Telecommunication, Computing, Electronics and Control) · Universitas Ahmad Dahlan · 2020

Applied VADER-based automatic labelling to social-media discourse surrounding the Christchurch attack; quantified islamophobia sentiment dynamics and validated the approach against manually labelled ground truth using Naive Bayes and SVM, with SMOTE for imbalanced data. 02

Sentiment Analysis about E-Commerce from Tweets Using Decision Tree, K-Nearest Neighbor, and Naive Bayes

IEEE · International Conference on Informatics, Computing, Engineering and Power Technology · 2019

Comparative study of three classical classifiers (Decision Tree, KNN, Naive Bayes) on Indonesian e-commerce tweet sentiment from Tokopedia and Bukalapak. Text mining techniques (tokenize, stem, classify) were applied in RapidMiner. Naive Bayes achieved the best result with 77% accuracy, 88.50% precision, and 64% recall.
